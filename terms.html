BEFORE YOU HEAD UP

Trip inquiry terms.

Last updated November 21, 2026.

Requests are not reservations

Submitting the form opens a prefilled email draft that you must send. An email inquiry does not create a reservation. Service, pickup location, vehicle capacity, trip details, payment methods, and any reservation are subject to written confirmation from the operator.

Lee Canyon package pricing

Lee Canyon round-trip packages are $299 total for one or two shuttle passengers, $349 total for three passengers, and $399 total for four passengers. These are total trip prices, not per-person prices. Rates are for the advertised Lee Canyon itinerary, and other destinations are quoted individually. No payment is collected through this website.

Included hosted Las Vegas show experience

Eligible confirmed Lee Canyon packages include a complimentary host-attended Las Vegas show admission for up to three invited shuttle passengers, if a specific eligible event and sufficient guest seats are available and confirmed before booking. The host attends the show and occupies one of the four show seats. If four passengers ride the shuttle, only three can receive the included show invitation and the fourth passenger receives shuttle transportation only. Show admission carries no separately stated charge and is not independently sold or transferable. A trip inquiry does not guarantee a show reservation or a particular event. We will disclose the confirmed show details and applicable guest rules before finalizing a package; if no suitable show is available, guests may decline or request a separately quoted transportation-only trip. Guest attendance is subject to applicable membership and venue rules.

Timing and conditions

The advertised Lee Canyon itinerary is a planned 8:00 AM Las Vegas departure and a planned 5:00 PM return departure from the mountain. Travel times and schedules can change due to traffic, weather, road conditions, restrictions, availability, or resort operations. No transportation is guaranteed by completing an inquiry.

Single-day lift tickets and other exclusions

Ski.Vegas is designed for individual day trips, not multiday ski packages. Round-trip transportation does not include Lee Canyon ski lift admission, rentals, lessons, food, or other resort services unless specifically stated in a confirmed booking. Each rider should purchase a daily lift ticket for the chosen date directly from the Lee Canyon official ticket store, and review current pricing, available terrain, and resort operations. Lift ticket prices vary by date and availability. Eligible hosted Las Vegas show admission is described separately above and is subject to confirmation.

Rain and mountain weather

Check the official forecast and resort conditions for your destination and travel date before confirming a trip. Rain, insufficient snow, and unfavorable destination weather are outside Vegas Ski & Snowboarding Shuttle's control. We provide transportation and are not responsible for rainy weather, other destination conditions, or a disappointing ski or sightseeing experience due to those conditions. Changes to transportation because of unsafe roads or weather, and any cancellations or refunds, are handled under the booking terms accepted before payment and applicable law.

Cancellation and refunds

Specific cancellation, weather disruption, refund and modification terms must be provided and accepted before a paid reservation. This information page does not replace the final booking agreement.

Independent operator

Vegas Ski & Snowboarding Shuttle is independent. This site does not claim to be an official service of Lee Canyon, Brian Head Resort, or Arizona Snowbowl.
