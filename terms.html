BEFORE YOU HEAD UP

Trip inquiry terms.

Last updated October 27, 2026.

Requests are not reservations

Submitting the form opens a prefilled email draft that you must send. An email inquiry does not create a reservation. Service, pickup location, vehicle capacity, trip details, payment methods, and any reservation are subject to written confirmation from the operator.

Pricing and promotion

The advertised planning rate for Lee Canyon is $200 per rider, round trip. Other destinations are quoted individually. Any $50-back promotion is subject to terms confirmed before payment; do not assume automatic eligibility, redemption, or a final $150 price. No payment is collected through this website.

Timing and conditions

The advertised Lee Canyon itinerary is a planned 8:00 AM Las Vegas departure and a planned 5:00 PM return departure from the mountain. Travel times and schedules can change due to traffic, weather, road conditions, restrictions, availability, or resort operations. No transportation is guaranteed by completing an inquiry.

What is not included

Unless expressly stated in an individually confirmed booking, lift tickets, gear rentals, lessons, food, and other resort services are not part of the transportation price. Guests should verify resort operations and purchase any needed lift tickets directly from the resort.

Cancellation and refunds

Specific cancellation, weather disruption, refund and modification terms must be provided and accepted before a paid reservation. This information page does not replace the final booking agreement.

Independent operator

Vegas Ski & Snowboarding Shuttle is independent. This site does not claim to be an official service of Lee Canyon, Brian Head Resort, or Arizona Snowbowl.
