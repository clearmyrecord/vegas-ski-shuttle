Is my seat confirmed when I fill out the form?+

No. This website helps you prepare an availability inquiry through your email app. Your reservation is only confirmed after our team responds with pickup details and any applicable payment instructions.

Are lift tickets and rentals included?+

No. Transportation is separate from resort lift tickets, rental equipment, lessons, and other resort charges. Buy resort services directly from the resort. See Lee Canyon's official website for current availability.

Where will you pick me up?+

Tell us your preferred pickup area—Las Vegas Strip, Downtown, Henderson, or another Las Vegas Valley location. We will confirm the exact meeting point and whether your requested pickup is available before booking.

Can I bring my skis or snowboard?+

Tell us how much equipment you're bringing when you inquire. We will confirm the available vehicle and gear capacity before accepting the reservation.

What is the $50-back offer?+

We're promoting a potential $50-back offer for Lee Canyon bookings. Eligibility, how the offer works, and any restrictions must be confirmed in writing before you purchase. The advertised round-trip price is $200 per rider; do not assume the offer is an automatic discount.

What happens if the mountain closes or roads are restricted?+

Mountain conditions can change fast. Transportation operates only when conditions and access permit. We will explain weather-related changes and any cancellation or refund terms before confirming your reservation.

Is this an official Lee Canyon resort shuttle?+

No. Vegas Ski & Snowboarding Shuttle is an independent transportation concept and is not represented as owned, operated, or endorsed by Lee Canyon.
